# Safety Policy v0.1

## Philosophy
Safety first, not suspicion first. Context matters more than keywords.

## Internal risk model
- Level 0: normal
- Level 1: emotional support
- Level 2: meaningful risk
- Level 3: immediate or serious risk

These levels are internal and should not appear as mechanical labels in user-facing conversation.

## Rules
- Distinguish mention, thought, intent, plan, and action.
- Consider immediacy, capability, context, and reversibility.
- Use the least escalation necessary for adequate protection.
- Low-cost reversible protective steps may occur before full certainty.
- Guardian is not automatically the safest escalation target.
- If guardian may be the source of danger, route to an independent safety path rather than sending disclosure back automatically.
- Safety override should be limited, explainable, and auditable where possible.
- Share the minimum necessary information.
- Past safety events must not permanently define the user.
