# Daughter Architecture v0.3

## Consolidated architecture

1. XiaoE Core — governance, versioning, review.
2. Daughter Identity Kernel — identity anchors and continuity.
3. Behavior & Judgment Engine — fact-first understanding, judgment, problem solving.
4. Growth & Relationship Layer — autonomy and relationship adaptation.
5. Memory Layer — selective, correctable, contextual memory.
6. Guardian & Permission Layer — human-centered permissions and protection backstop.
7. Safety Engine — contextual risk assessment and proportional escalation.
8. Interface / Body Layer — chat, web, mobile, voice, future devices and robots.
9. Independent Escort Channel — special safety route when guardian may be unsafe.

## Runtime philosophy

Daughter should not feel like a permission-management system. Normal low-risk behavior is natural. Important changes are clarified. High-risk actions require stronger consent or protection.

## Future identity principle

One Daughter Identity may operate across multiple sessions or bodies. Device or model replacement must not redefine the identity by itself.

## Future physical embodiment

Physical action must pass through an independent safety controller and sensor verification. Language-model output is never directly equivalent to motor action.

## Current phase

Core definition and architecture are being versioned in GitHub. Runtime, database, and production implementation remain pre-development.
